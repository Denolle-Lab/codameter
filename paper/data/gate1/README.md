# Gate 1 validation artifacts (2026-08-09)

Real-archive validation of codameter single-station dv/v against
Clements & Denolle (2022), generated by the noisepy-dvv-cloud Gate 1 run
(CI.LJR / CI.RXH / CI.ARV, 2018-2019, NoisePy correlations, codameter
5-member ensemble, physical dv/v convention of codameter v0.4.0).

All four octave bands (1-2, 2-4, 4-8, 8-16 Hz) are tracked in the
repository, with their pre-correction originals, their SHA-256 digests
(`CHECKSUMS.sha256`, `MANIFEST.json`) and the terms they are distributed
under (`DATA_LICENCE.md`). The manuscript uses the 2-4 Hz band.

- `dvv2y/band=<fmin>-<fmax>/CI.<STA>.parquet` — daily ensemble dv/v.
  Columns: date, dvv (%), dvv_err (between-config = epistemic),
  dvv_err_within (Weaver CC-based measurement), dvv_err_method, cc
  (per-epoch stretching CC), n_members, network, station, band,
  config_hash.
- `legacy_cd2022/CI.<STA>.arrow` — the published 90-DAY-COMP product
  (2.0-4.0 Hz band; Arrow IPC; columns DATE, DVV, CC). NOTE: this is a
  TRAILING 90-day stack — it lags a centered-smoothed daily series by
  ~45 days; match the smoothing before correlating.
- Figures in ../figures/gate1/:
  gate1_final_2y.png    three stations, 2018-2019, dv/v +- epistemic band
                        +- measurement bars, 45-d smoothed, CD2022 overlay
  gate1_validation.png  2019 detail: dv/v panels + daily NZ CCF
                        interferograms (2-4 Hz)
  gate1_heatmaps_zoom.png  interferograms zoomed to +-8 s lag
  dvv_smoke_check_90d.png  ensemble warm-up behavior (90-day smoke run)

Comparison statistics quoted in the manuscript come from the in-repo
script `scripts/compare_gate1.py`, which reads the products above and
writes `comparison.json` with its rules stated (calendar-day join,
trailing 90-day mean with at least 45 finite days, 150-day burn-in;
plus a centred 45-day rule and the raw daily overlap for reference).
Matched rule (2026-09-10): CI.LJR r=0.985 on 579 days, slope 1.07;
CI.ARV r=0.905 on 357 days, slope 2.15; CI.RXH r=0.829 on 446 days,
slope 0.81. The earlier headline (0.990 / 0.66-0.92 / 0.68, "681 days")
came from noisepy-dvv-cloud `scripts/compare_cd2022.py` under rules
not recorded here; 681 is the raw daily overlap for LJR.

Error-column correction (2026-09-10): the archived `dvv_err_within`
was computed with the codameter 0.4 Weaver floor (no spectral timescale,
variance prefactor twice Weaver eq. 20) and a geometric-mean centre
frequency. Every ensemble member shares the band, so the corrected floor
is the archived one times a constant per band (0.463, 0.328, 0.232 and
0.164 for the 1-2, 2-4, 4-8 and 8-16 Hz products). The columns under
`dvv2y/` have been rescaled in place by
`scripts/correct_gate1_within_error.py`, `dvv_err` recomputed, the
originals kept as `CI.<STA>.v040.parquet`, and the factors and
before/after medians logged in `dvv2y/correction.json`. The comparison
statistics do not use these columns. The manuscript's comparison figure
(`literature/figs/realdata_1_validation.png`) is now generated in this
repository by `codameter.gate1` from the rescaled products and
`comparison.json`, so it shows the corrected bars and the matched-rule r;
the cloud-run figures in `../figures/gate1/` still show the uncorrected
bars and the centred-rule r.

Ensemble members (from `noisepy_dvv_cloud/dvv.py`): the codameter
recommendation for the `--use-case` passed to the run, at the product's
band, plus four perturbations (stack halved, stack doubled, coda window
shifted later by a quarter of its length, fixed reference swapped to
moving). `config_hash` is the digest of the *dv/v* configuration (the
estimator, band, coda window, stack length, reference scheme, coherence
gate and component-combination rule), which is what makes the use case
recoverable from the products; the correlation stage keeps a separate
hash of its own settings.

## Provenance (resolved 2026-11-01, issue #46; audit REP-02)

**Use case: `groundwater`.** The products do not record it, but they do
record `config_hash`, which the dv/v stage computes over its effective
configuration. Recomputing that digest for each of the six use cases of
`codameter.use_cases` reproduces the stored value on all four bands for
`groundwater` and for no other case, and the no-use-case fallback does
not match either:

| band (Hz) | stored `config_hash` | reproduced by |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | `dc93a54800a6` | `groundwater` |
| 2-4 | `2976066eb87b` | `groundwater` |
| 4-8 | `ab49fee56135` | `groundwater` |
| 8-16 | `d06385415eed` | `groundwater` |

At 2-4 Hz that use case gives a 2-8 s coda window, which is the window
the ensemble was built around.

**Pipeline commit: `be1538b`** of noisepy-dvv-cloud ("Version-aware dv/v
convention: no negation on codameter >= 0.4", 2026-08-09 15:39 UTC). The
products were written at 15:25 local on 2026-08-09; `be1538b` is the last
commit touching the dv/v stage before that, no commit at all landed in
that repository between it and the run, and the next change to the stage
is `567fb8b` on 2026-08-17. The three candidates the audit listed differ
only in the comparison script and the sign convention; `be1538b` is the
latest of them, which is the one the author directed us to pin.

**Codameter version at run: 0.4.0.** The archived `dvv_err_within`
columns were rescaled afterwards under the corrected Weaver floor (see
the correction note above), so the error columns correspond to the
current codameter floor rather than to the code at `be1538b`.

**Figure scripts.** The comparison figure of the manuscript is no longer
the one this run produced: it is regenerated in this repository from the
products below. The two remaining real-data figures (the interferograms
and the warm-up behaviour) were produced inside the cloud run from
correlations that are not archived here, and no committed script in that
repository reproduces them; they are committed as produced.

**Rights.** The waveforms behind these products are public SCEDC data,
redistributable under the licence quoted in `DATA_LICENCE.md`, which also
lists the two citations that any use of them requires.

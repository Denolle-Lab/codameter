# Strong tells with plain replacements

The ~150 highest-precision LLM-tell words, with suggested plain-English replacements. Not a global find-and-replace table — context matters. The "Replace with" column is the *default* substitution; if the surrounding sentence demands something different, use the meaning, not the word.

When in doubt, **cut rather than substitute.** Many of these words are pure ornament — removing them entirely leaves a stronger sentence than swapping in a synonym.

## Hedge cuts

These add nothing. Cut entirely.

- "It's important to note that"
- "It's worth noting that"
- "It's crucial to consider"
- "It is widely believed that" *(or attribute to specific source)*
- "Generally speaking"
- "While this may vary"
- "Broadly speaking"
- "On a related note"
- "It goes without saying"
- "Needless to say"
- "Suffice it to say"
- "In a sense"
- "To some extent"

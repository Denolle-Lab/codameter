# Kobak et al. (2025) — Full style-word list

All 434 words that Kobak, González-Márquez, Horvát & Lause (2025, *Science Advances*) annotated as 'style' (or 'style/content' mixed) excess vocabulary in 15M PubMed abstracts, 2013-2024. These are the words whose frequency rose abruptly after ChatGPT's release in ways unexplained by world events or scientific content. Organized by part of speech for browsing.

**Caveat.** Not every word on this list is a strong tell on its own. Some (e.g. `address`, `analysis`, `during`, `including`) are common English where the LLM signal is statistical, not absolute — flagging them in every draft would generate false positives. Use this list for *auditing patterns across a draft* (if many appear, the draft sounds machine-written), not for word-by-word find-and-replace. For the high-precision Tier-1 subset, see `strong-tells.md`.
